# Application screenshots

Captured from the real Kabacus web app at source revision
b9e049cd52d6c5aef904f2a7e27fa1b4c1998471, built with the red abacus icon
and the Add chooser fix that follow it. The app was served by a
`kabacus-server` from that revision, in `--auth=none` mode, on scratch
books made for the purpose. No private financial data is shown.

The sample books are a fictional household: Checking and Savings, two
reserves (Car insurance, Winter trip), an Everyday Visa with a matching
card reserve, a car loan, and $42.08 a friend owes for shared groceries.
Payees (Maple Market, Brightline Logistics, Cedar Mutual and so on) are
invented. Transactions run from September 1 to November 3, 2026, and the
browser clock was fixed at November 14, 2026. On that day Cash on Hand is
$18,288.84: Money on Hand $20,067.67, less Future Allocations $1,736.75
and Receivables $42.08.

Desktop screens are 1440 × 1000 at 1× (`credit.webp` is cropped to 1440 × 640); `phone-overview.webp` is a
390 × 844 phone at 2× (780 × 1688). PNG captures are encoded as WebP
(quality 82).
